# The Keeper of the Last Lamp

### A. FILM OVERVIEW

**Title:** The Keeper of the Last Lamp
**Genre:** Emotional sci-fi
**Duration:** 36 seconds (final runtime will follow the recorded narration)
**Total scenes:** 6
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9
**Visual style:** Photorealistic cinematic
**Narrator:** Female, late fifties, calm and low, unhurried, a little weathered; speaks like she is remembering, not performing; slow pace with small pauses before the last line.
**Logline:** On a frozen coast where no ship has come for forty years, an old keeper lights the lamp every night, until one night something answers.
**Synopsis:** Ada keeps the last lighthouse on a coast that froze when the oceans stopped. Every night she climbs the tower and lights the lamp, though no ship has come in forty years.

One night, far out on the ice, a small light answers hers. Then another. Out of the dark comes a line of sledges, pulled by children, following her light home.

She was never keeping the lamp for ships. She was keeping it so someone would know where home was.
**Central theme:** Keeping faith for people you have never met.
