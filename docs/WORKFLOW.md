# How a video gets made

Each step ends with something the person can see.

1. **Brief.** What is the video, who watches it, how long, where it goes. Collect what exists: script, voiceover or recorded
   video, screen recordings, a reference video, brand colours. Agree the background colour.
2. **Timing from their voice.** If there is a voiceover or a talking-head edit, get word timings for it (any word-level
   transcript). Every graphic is timed to the words, so this comes before design.
3. **Beat plan.** Split the script into beats (one idea each). Per beat: the words, what is on screen (what each key word
   names), the layout, the 1 to 3 word key phrase, the sounds. Keep neighbouring beats visually different.
4. **Stills first.** For a new direction, render 2 to 4 still frames and show them before building motion.
5. **Build.** One composition per scene in `compositions/`, on the animated background, with the pieces in `docs/LIBRARY.md`.
6. **Check** (`check-before-sending` skill). Fix, check again.
7. **Render and review.** Render each scene (`--fps 60 --quality high --video-frame-format png`), join them, add sound, send
   the review video. Apply notes, check again, send again.
8. **Deliver** the MP4, or put the graphics over their footage in DaVinci Resolve (`resolve-overlay` skill).
9. **Clean up** drafts once they have the final.
