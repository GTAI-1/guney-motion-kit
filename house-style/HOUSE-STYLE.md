# House style (starter edition)

Read this before the first creative decision of a video when `houseStyle` is on. It is a starting point: change any rule
the person asks you to, and write their own preferences here as you learn them.

## Readable on a phone
- Key phrases 120 px or bigger, labels 40 px or bigger, high contrast on the dark background. If in doubt, bigger.
- One key phrase per beat, 1 to 3 words, on one line (never wrap it).
- Fewer, larger elements beat many small ones.

## Motion
- The background moves for the whole video, so no frame ever looks frozen.
- Things build on screen one at a time: each element arrives on its own, already filled in, and lands before the next.
  Nothing pops in, nothing waits empty.
- Entrances are quick and smooth (0.3 to 0.6 s, easing out), exits leave in a direction. No bouncing.
- Show what each key word names: when the voice says "folder", a folder appears.
- Neighbouring beats look different: change the layout, the element or the direction.

## Colour
- One main colour (the background colour) plus one second colour for contrast. Brand colours stay their own colour.

## People on camera
- Keep graphics clear of the face, and leave the speaker's half of the frame empty in a split layout.
- Nothing is cut by the edges of the frame.

## Sound
- Soft clicks for keys and buttons, a quiet whoosh for big moves, used sparingly. The voice is always clearest.

## Before showing anything
- Look at the frames (the `check-before-sending` skill): first frame, last frame, every change of layout. Fix, then show.
